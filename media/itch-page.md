## Description

A small orange 4x4. Five valleys of Ukraine. Cargo that has to get through.

**Khutir Road** is a PSX-style off-road delivery game. Start in a snowbound valley at dusk,
hauling groceries to the lone *khutir* (a Ukrainian farmstead), and earn your way on: a sunny
autumn steppe, Zakarpattia's spring vineyards under a castle, a foggy Polissia thaw with a road
across a frozen lake, and a Carpathian pass at night in a blizzard.

- **Five valleys**, each with its own ground, places and sky, and the sky yours to switch: sun,
  overcast, snow or rain, night.
- **A job board at the depot:** race the clock for gold, nurse a fragile load over the bumps,
  or winch a stranger's car out of a ditch.
- **A garage of rigs:** a trailer for the pickup, a heavy army 6x6 for the concrete rings, a farm
  tractor and its tipper trailer for grain through the bog, a semi truck with a 12 m flatbed for
  logs and pipes. Back up to a trailer and hitch it yourself.
- **Deep snow and mud** fight every wheel, and pack down into ruts that stay where you left them.
- **Drive like it matters:** all-wheel drive, a differential lock and low range, each with its
  moment.
- **The winch:** stuck? Hook the nearest pine or rock and reel yourself out.
- **A paper map** with contour lines and the trail you have driven.
- **Hear it work:** the diesel labours through its gears, the tyres crunch snow and squelch mud.
- **Watch the fuel**, or pay for the tow back to the depot.
- 480x270, wobbly vertices, dithering and fog, like it fell out of 1998.

F winch (hold Shift to reel in) ·
M map · T tow · R flip upright · right mouse to orbit the camera, wheel to zoom · F1 help ·
Esc the valleys
