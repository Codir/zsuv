## Description

A small orange 4x4. A snowbound valley at dusk. Cargo that has to get through.

**Khutir Road** is a PSX-style off-road delivery game. Load up at the depot and haul groceries to
the lone *khutir* (a Ukrainian farmstead), spare parts to the sawmill and drone batteries to the
radio tower on the hill.

- **Deep snow and mud** fight every wheel, and pack down into ruts that stay where you left them.
- **Drive like it matters:** all-wheel drive, a differential lock and low range, each with its
  moment.
- **The winch:** stuck? Hook the nearest pine or rock and reel yourself out.
- **A paper map** with contour lines and the trail you have driven.
- **Hear it work:** the diesel labours through its gears, the tyres crunch snow and squelch mud.
- **Watch the fuel**, or pay for the tow back to the depot.
- 480x270, wobbly vertices, dithering and fog, like it fell out of 1998.

### Controls

W/S throttle/brake · A/D steer · Space handbrake · Q 4x4 · X diff lock · G low range ·
H headlights · E load/unload/refuel · F winch (hold Shift to reel in) · M map · T tow ·
R flip upright · right mouse to orbit the camera, wheel to zoom · F1 help

An early prototype made with Godot. Feedback welcome!
