# Khutir Road 0.1.2: Hear it work

*The itch.io devlog post for 0.1.2 (Edit game → Devlog → Create a devlog post). Attach
`media/screenshots/04-mud-4x4-diff-lock.png`.*

---

The valley isn't silent any more, and the controls step out of the way: both straight from Philip's comment in the jam.

**The engine works for it.** The diesel climbs through its gears and drops back at every shift; in low range it revs harder for the same crawl. Floor it where the truck can't go and you hear it growl; let a wheel spin in the snow and the revs flare. Run the tank dry and it dies.

**Snow and mud under the tyres.** Every wheel crunches through the snow and squelches through the mud as it rolls, louder the deeper it gets. Standing still, they're quiet.

**The winch.** Its motor whines while it reels you in, and sinks lower as the cable takes the strain.

**The controls stay, the view comes back.** The card of every key no longer covers the view: a three-line hint of the keys you drive with sits in the top right corner. F1 shows every key, and F1 again hides it all.

**The sounds are real recordings**, all CC0: a diesel truck, footsteps in snow and a little electric winch by Joseph SARDIN of BigSoundBank, and mud squelches by rubberduck on OpenGameArt. Thank you both.

**Next up.** More valleys to deliver to and new kinds of jobs, as Philip asked: that's 0.2.0.

Thanks to everyone who left a comment: they really do shape the next update :0)
