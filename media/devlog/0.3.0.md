# Khutir Road 0.3.0: Trucks and trailers

*The itch.io devlog post for 0.3.0 (Edit game → Devlog → Create a devlog post). Attach a shot of
the four trucks side by side and one of the semi with logs on a mountain road.*

---

The orange pickup has company in the garage: a heavy 6x6, a farm tractor and a semi truck, and three trailers to pull behind them, as in SnowRunner.

**Four new rigs.** A trailer for the pickup, for a double load. A heavy army 6x6 on big lugged tyres that ploughs through the deep snow where the pickup bogs down, and the only one strong enough for the concrete rings. A farm tractor with a tipper trailer: slow, but it crawls through the Polissia bog without even needing 4x4. And a blue semi truck with a 12 m flatbed: the fastest thing on a road, a handful in a hairpin.

**The garage.** Press Tab on the job board. Everything is bought once from the same wallet as the valleys, and stays yours in every valley: the trailer for $800, the tractor and its trailer for $2500, the 6x6 for $3500, the semi truck and its semi-trailer for $5000. Pick a truck there and it waits for you at the depot.

**Hitching up.** Your trailers wait beside the depot. Back up to one and press C; press it again to leave it standing, say while you winch yourself out of a ditch. The trailer turns and pitches on its hitch, brakes when you brake, and waits for you by the depot when the tow truck takes you back.

**More jobs.** The board now shows five, at least two for the truck you're in, and tells you what the rest need. Concrete rings are the 6x6's now, and pay twice. New loads: a double load in the pickup and its trailer, grain, peat or hay in the farm trailer, logs and pipes on the semi-trailer, the best paid of all if you can get them up the mountain.
