# 0.1.0 — The road crew

*The Devlog tab of https://codirs.itch.io/zhuv, after the deploy. Attach a screenshot of the tractor
digging, once `media/screenshots/` has one.*

Spring after the floods: the land has shifted, and the road to the upper village is gone. This is
the first build of Zsuv for GitHub Game Off 2026.

**A map that draws itself.** Five biomes of Ukraine on one map: the steppe, a snowy pine forest
across the river, the Polissia swamp, the Zakarpattia foothills and a Carpathian pass. The paper
map is blank until you drive it. The little scout sees furthest; the heavy trucks follow its track.

**Shift the land.** The tractor's bucket bites the ground with B and fills its tipper; V tips it
out. Fill the washout on the road to the river, dig out the landslide, spread gravel into a road
across the bog.

**Build where you choose.** Fuel stations, sawmills, quarries, a concrete plant, outposts: press P,
put the footprint down where it fits, and haul in what it needs. There is no money. Fuel runs out,
so the next fuel station is what gets you further.

**Concrete sets.** The mixer's load hardens in five minutes. Is the road good enough to make it to
the bridge in time?

**Trucks that stay.** Every truck stays where you leave it. Step across with E, winch one out with
another, aim the winch with the mouse. Deep water floods an engine without a snorkel.

Built on my earlier game Khutir Road (October 2026): the driving, the trucks and the sound come
from it. Everything else was made for the jam. Feedback welcome!
