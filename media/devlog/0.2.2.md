# Khutir Road 0.2.2: Five valleys

*The itch.io devlog post for 0.2.2 (Edit game → Devlog → Create a devlog post). Attach the
valleys screen and two of `previews/` (the steppe and the Carpathian pass). It is the first build
since 0.1.1, so it also tells of 0.1.2's sound and controls hint, whose own post was never put up.*

---

The khutir valley has company: four more valleys to earn your way into, and a board of jobs at every depot. Both came from Philip's comment in the jam: more maps and challenges. And you can hear the truck now, too.

**Five valleys.** The snowbound khutir valley at dusk is where you start. Then a sunny autumn steppe with long dry dirt roads, gullies and a hill of wind turbines; Zakarpattia in the spring sun, vineyards on the slopes and a castle on its hill; Polissia on a foggy morning in the thaw, where the road to the fish farm runs across a frozen lake; and the Carpathian pass at night in a blizzard, switchbacks through deep snow up to a weather station on the ridge. Each one has more places to deliver to than the one before.

**Pick your valley, and its sky.** The game opens on the valleys: a picture of each and what awaits there. Buy the next one with what you've earned. Each valley has its own weather, but you can switch it: the steppe at night, the pass in the sun.

**The job board.** Stop at the depot and press E: three jobs, each of a different kind. Race the clock for gold, silver or bronze and beat your own record. Nurse a fragile load over the bumps: every hard landing breaks a bit more of it, and you're paid for what arrives whole. Haul concrete rings that sit the truck low and drink the tank. Or drive out to a car that slid into the ditch, hook your winch on it and pull it back onto the road while your truck holds still. Changed your mind? A new job at the depot drops the one you hold.

**Hear it work.** Also from Philip: the diesel climbs through its gears and drops back at every shift, growls when you floor it and go nowhere, and flares when a wheel spins; the tyres crunch through snow and squelch through mud, louder the deeper it gets; the winch whines lower under the strain. Real recordings, free for anyone (CC0): a diesel truck, footsteps in snow and an electric winch by Joseph SARDIN (BigSoundBank), mud squelches by rubberduck (OpenGameArt).

**The controls step aside.** A three-line hint in the corner instead of the whole card of keys; F1 brings the card back.

**Next up.** Different trucks for different loads, and trailers: a heavy 6x6, a tractor unit with a semi-trailer, a tractor with a trailer for the bogs, and a trailer for the pickup.

Thanks to everyone who left a comment: they really do shape the next update :0)
