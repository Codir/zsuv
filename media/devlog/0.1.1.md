# 0.1.1 — How to dig, and where the timber is

*The Devlog tab of https://codirs.itch.io/zhuv, after the deploy. Attach
`media/screenshots/03-the-bucket-bites.png`.*

Two things nobody could work out from the game alone, now said on screen, and a winch that works
everywhere.

**How to dig.** In the tractor the game tells you: hold B and creep forward, and the bucket bites
the ground into the tipper. Too fast and it says so. At the washout with soil aboard, hold V to
tip it in.

**Where the timber is.** The first sawmill needs timber, and the railhead's yard starts with four
loads. The build list now says where every need comes from, and the old sawmill across the river
comes back for one load of parts.

**The winch in the forest.** Past the steppe the winch aimed at a point far away and would not
hook. It hooks every tree and rock now.

**Less clutter.** The yellow arrow over the truck is gone, and the winch's marker shows only while
you aim it.
