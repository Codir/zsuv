# Khutir Road 0.1.1: Hands off

*The itch.io devlog post for 0.1.1 (Edit game → Devlog → Create a devlog post). Attach
`media/screenshots/05-deep-snow.png`.*

---

A small fix straight from the jam's comments: when you let go of W, the truck lets go of the throttle.

**No more runaway throttle in the browser.** smokeyai found that if you held W and clicked outside the game, the browser never told the game you had let go: the truck sat bogged in the snow with the engine still pulling, burning its fuel with nothing pressed. Now the game lets go of every key the moment it loses the keyboard.

**Next up.** Philip asked for a controls hint that does not cover the view, for sound (the engine labouring in the snow, the tyres crunching through it) and for more of the valley to deliver to. All three are on the bench.

Thanks to everyone who left a comment: they really do shape the next update :0)
