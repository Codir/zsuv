## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-14 — The road crew

The first build for GitHub Game Off 2026 (theme SHIFT): a new game on Khutir Road's base. The plan
is `docs/plan.md`.

- **One map of five biomes:** the steppe, a snowy pine forest across the river, the Polissia swamp,
  the Zakarpattia foothills and the Carpathian pass, joined at their borders. The paper map starts
  blank and draws itself as you drive; the scout sees furthest, and its track is kept.
- **The goal:** the upper village, cut off in the mountains, calls on the radio. Bring it fuel and
  parts along a road you made, and the end card counts what you shifted and built.
- **Shift the land:** the tractor's bucket digs with B into its tipper, V tips it. Fill the washout,
  dig out the landslide, level a spot, spread gravel into a road. The ground wears where heavy
  trucks spin in mud.
- **Build where you choose:** P places fuel stations, sawmills, quarries, concrete plants and
  outposts, green or red with the reason; E brings them their loads. Bridges at the old crossings,
  gravel roads and fills. Production runs while you are away. No money: everything is loads and
  fuel, and the railhead's yard starts you off.
- **The trucks:** a new scout that carries nothing and sees far, and a concrete mixer whose load
  sets in five minutes. Every truck stays where you leave it: step across with E, or pick one on the
  map. The 6x6 and the semi wait out on the map to be found.
- **The winch aimed with the mouse:** F, then click the tree, rock, building or truck to hook.
- **Water:** drag, the river's current, and an engine that floods over its intake. The garage's
  workshop (U) makes a snorkel, chains, a long winch and the 6x6's bodies from parts, and repairs.
- **A gearbox you shift:** N for manual, `,` and `.` to shift; first gear of low range crawls where
  auto spins.
- A title screen with Continue and New game; the save is `user://zsuv.cfg`, apart from Khutir
  Road's.
